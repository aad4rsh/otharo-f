Othäro Incubation Program

Othäro is a 6-month startup incubation program by King's College Nepal that helps early-stage ventures grow into sustainable businesses through mentorship, expert sessions, practical workshops, networking, and access to a supportive entrepreneurial ecosystem.

Charcuterie & Conversations

Charcuterie & Conversations is a networking experience that brings entrepreneurs, mentors, and professionals together for meaningful conversations, and fun. The event combines casual networking, interactive activities, and a relaxed atmosphere to help participants build genuine relationships beyond the traditional business setting.

R2B: Research to Business

R2B (Research to Business) is a pre-incubation program by King's College Nepal that helps transform promising research and innovative ideas into market-ready ventures. Through mentorship, validation, business development, and entrepreneurial support, R2B bridges the gap between research and real-world impact.

Popcorn Series

Popcorn Series is an ongoing knowledge-sharing initiative that brings entrepreneurs, industry experts, and inspiring changemakers together to share insights, experiences, and lessons from their journeys. It offers practical perspectives and fresh ideas to inspire and empower the startup community.

Startup Garage

Startup Garage is a hands-on support initiative that helps entrepreneurs test, validate, and strengthen their startup ideas through expert guidance, practical insights, and one-on-one consultations. It connects founders with experienced professionals across key areas such as finance, legal, and marketing.

About Othāro Incubation Center

For over a decade, Othāro—King's College Nepal's startup incubator—has provided early-stage founders with the mentorship, market strategies, and ecosystem access needed to launch sustainable businesses. We help turn your vision into a market-ready reality.
