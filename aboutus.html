Otharo History

The Birth of CINED

2013

King's College Nepal launched its first innovation cell, laying the foundation for student-led entrepreneurship in Kathmandu.

King's Incubator

2015

CINED evolved into King's Incubator, pairing early-stage founders with mentorship, workspace, and seed support.

Structured Growth

2016

Ad-hoc support became structured programs — validation sprints, investor clinics, and a formal alumni network.

The Yunus Partnership

2017

A partnership with Yunus Social Business brought global best practices and widened access to impact capital.

Otharo Excellence

TODAY

Otharo plays on — accelerating startups, building community, and turning ambitious ideas into sustainable businesses.

About Othāro Incubation Center

For over a decade, Othāro—King's College Nepal's startup incubator—has provided early-stage founders with the mentorship, market strategies, and ecosystem access needed to launch sustainable businesses. We help turn your vision into a market-ready reality.
