OTHARO - ओथारो

"Otharo" (ओथारो) is the Nepali word for incubation / brooding — the gentle, dedicated care given to nurture an egg until it hatches into life. At Otharo, King's College incubation wing, we provide early-stage dreamers, students, and founders with seed-bed resources, rigorous validation, workspace, and 1-on-1 industry mentorship to build sustainable companies.
